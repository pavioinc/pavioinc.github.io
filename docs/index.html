The payment loop

From invoice to receipt, without a follow-up call

This is the core flow Pavio was built around first — the part of running a PG that eats the most time.

1

Invoice generated

Rent cycles run automatically, or an owner records a one-off charge in seconds.

2

Payment link sent

A Razorpay link goes to the tenant — or the owner records cash/UPI paid in person.

3

Confirmed automatically

Webhooks reconcile the payment the moment it clears — no manual matching against a bank statement.

4

Receipt & notification

A PDF receipt is generated and a WhatsApp/SMS confirmation goes out — to owner and tenant, automatically.
